Access that moves

Nentric Flow connects the resident, host, security team and visitor around one clear access event. It gives estates, offices and communities a shared view of who is arriving, what has been approved and what should happen next. Nentric focuses on the points where people, permission, information and physical movement meet, then builds the interface around the sequence people actually experience.

How it works

The product follows the journey.

What is Nentric and what do we do?

Nentric is a technology company that builds digital infrastructure to manage how people, access, and responsibilities connect across different environments. At the core is the Nentric engine, a framework that consistently answers key questions: who, where, what, why, when, and what happens next.

This engine powers live solutions across sectors, from workplace technology to hospitality and restaurants, ensuring seamless flows of access, approval, and movement. The same foundation adapts to each setting, enabling Nentric to deliver reliable products today while expanding into new directions as they mature.

Is my data safe with Nentric?

Nentric only collects what a workflow needs to function, such as visitor and access details for Flow. The site does not run marketing or analytics cookies, and there is a plain language privacy notice on the site explaining what is collected and why.

Can I customize the platform for my specific community needs?

Yes! Nentric is designed to be flexible and customizable. You can configure access controls, communication channels, and features based on your community's unique requirements. Our team also provides setup assistance to ensure the platform works perfectly for your specific use case.

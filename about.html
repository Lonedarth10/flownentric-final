About Nentric

We buildwhat moves.

Nentric Technologies Ltd is a Nigerian technology company building practical, secure and scalable systems for environments where people, permission, information and physical movement meet.

The company

We look for complicated everyday journeys and build technology that makes them easier to understand, easier to manage and easier to trust.

Why Nentric exists

Technology should make important work feel simpler.

Nentric builds products around real environments rather than abstract workflows. That means starting with the people involved, the permissions they need, the place where something happens and what needs to be known next.

Our first products are Nentric Flow, Nentric Stay and Nentric Command. Together, they explore connected access, residential experiences and institutional operations through one consistent way of thinking.

We care about useful software, thoughtful design and the details that make a system dependable in the real world.

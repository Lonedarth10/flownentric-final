Privacy Policy.

How Nentric handles information connected to this website and enquiries.

Information we receive

We may receive information you choose to provide when you contact Nentric, such as your name, work email, organisation, operating environment and message. Basic technical information may also be received by the website when a browser connects to it.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, maintain the website, improve the experience, protect the service from misuse and meet lawful obligations.

Sharing and retention

Nentric does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers supporting the website or communications where necessary. Information is retained only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, business records, dispute resolution, security or legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask about personal information you have provided or request correction of inaccurate information through the Contact page.
